ORDERSIM

Orders fire from the POS to every station on their own. Bump finished tickets, tear receipts before they bury the screens, tear labels, and keep the printers from jamming.

SELECT SCENARIO
HOW IT PLAYS

Each station screen shows only the items it cooks. A ticket turns green when it is done cooking. Tap it to bump it out the door. Leave it sitting and it turns orange, then red, and your streak resets.

Every bump prints receipts. They stack up from the bottom of each station screen and cover the tickets. Tap a receipt to tear it off. Too many in one pile and that printer jams.

The expo printer and the label printer need tearing too. Jams, overdue tickets, and loose labels drain throughput. When throughput hits empty, the kitchen fails.
