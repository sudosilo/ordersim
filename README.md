ORDERSIM

ORDERSIM is a game for anyone curious what a busy restaurant kitchen feels like.

You enter a callsign and pick a scenario, breakfast rush, dinner rush, huge orders, or freeplay. Orders fire from the register to every kitchen station on their own. You bump finished tickets, tear receipts and labels, and clear printer jams. Progress saves in the browser and scores go to an online leaderboard.

Live at ordersim.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.
